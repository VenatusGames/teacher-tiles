PURPOSEFUL BY DESIGN. GUIDED BY YOU.

Productive Screen Time.
Purpose Comes First.

Give screen time an explicit purpose. Our focus is on avoiding screen fatigue and passive viewing: use the tools that support a clear learning or classroom goal, then make room for learning beyond the screen.

01

A Clear Job for Every Tool.

Practice a skill, explain a task, plan a lesson, or guide a routine. Choose each tool for what it helps your class accomplish, with a clear reason to bring it onto the screen.

02

Simple from the Start.

Start with a clear, focused workspace and just the tools you need. Add more only when it helps. Your board grows when you choose.

03

More Personality. Only by Choice.

Themes, animations, decorative effects, and busier graphics are optional. You decide what supports your students and your lesson, and how much visual activity belongs on the board.
