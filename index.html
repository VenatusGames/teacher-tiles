TeacherTiles controls at a glance

Keyboard shortcuts and mouse controls for moving quickly around your board.

⌕ COMING SOON
⌨
Keyboard shortcutsShortcuts are ignored while you are actively typing when appropriate.
Ctrl/⌘+A
Select all tiles and stickers; press again to clear.
Ctrl/⌘+C
Copy the current board selection.
Ctrl/⌘+V
Paste copied tiles or stickers.
Ctrl/⌘+D
Duplicate the current selection.
Ctrl/⌘+Z
Undo the latest board action.
Ctrl/⌘+Y
Redo an undone action. Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z also works.
Delete/Backspace
Delete only the selected tile or sticker—even when it belongs to a snapped group.
↑↓←→
Navigate around the board.
Shift+Drag
Temporarily disable tile snapping while you drag.
Esc
Exit text editing or close the active overlay/menu.
↖
Mouse & trackpadThe board is designed to stay fast without switching tools.
↔

Pan the boardLeft-drag empty board space or middle-mouse drag anywhere on the board.

⇧

Group selectHold Shift and left-drag empty space to draw a selection box.

☰

Add tilesRight-click empty board space to open the Add Tile menu.

⌁

ZoomScroll over the board for fast zoom. Hold Shift while scrolling for precise 1% steps.

✥

Move tilesDrag anywhere on a tile that is not an active button, slider, canvas, or other control.

⧉

Snap & groupPlace one tile against another to snap them into a group. Grouped tiles move together and share one layer.

↝

Hold, then tugPress and hold a grouped tile until it shakes, then pull through the resistance to detach and move it independently.

I

Edit textDouble-click a text field to type. Click away from it to leave text-edit mode.

◌

Transform stickersUse corner handles to resize and the round handle to rotate. Hold Shift while rotating to snap by 15°.

⌫

Delete by draggingDrag any snapped tile into the corner trash to delete its entire group.

○

Clear selectionClick outside the current selection to deselect it.
