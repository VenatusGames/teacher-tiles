# Reviewed classroom content

The unrestricted Wiktionary and Wikiquote feeds are removed. Long words and keyword filters did not provide a reliable classroom review.

The source of displayed definitions is the TeacherTiles classroom catalog: 365 advanced vocabulary entries with individually authored, plain-language definitions. Cambridge Dictionary links are lookup references, not a claim that Cambridge supplied or licensed these definitions. Vocabulary covers advanced academic language and science, including perspicacious, assiduous, equanimity and ephemeral. The default All collection gives 365 distinct daily selections; the optional subject collections are smaller and repeat sooner. Another previews the next entry and does not create an infinite stream.

The quote catalog contains 365 distinct public-domain excerpts about learning, effort, curiosity, kindness and friendship. Novel dialogue is credited to the author and work. Historical anthology attributions are explicitly linked to the collection in which they were printed; see ../quote-of-the-day/REVIEW.md for attribution limits. No raw upstream quote is eligible for automatic display. Expansion requires reviewing the exact quotation, attribution, source and classroom context.

Both tiles use stable hash-based permutations, independent of file or response order. Consecutive calendar days and Another walk through this mixed order. Reloading or switching between live and bundled content preserves today's entry. Each default collection completes a 365-day cycle before repeating, including when a cycle crosses daylight-saving transitions or February 29. A 366-day leap year needs a 366th entry to avoid a repeat on its last day.

Each catalog.json is fetched from this site's own origin daily. The bundled words.js and quotes.js contain the matching offline entries and form an exact-content allowlist; altered or unknown entries cannot appear through a catalog or cached response. Update both JSON and its bundled counterpart together after reviewing additions, and bump the shared daily cache key and script versions. Content refresh retrieves published catalog revisions, not automatically generated or externally sourced unreviewed entries. The reviewed-v2 cache ignores previous open-feed caches and the former 20-quote collection.

Google opens normal results in a separate tab with SafeSearch active. New tiles are 560 by 190 pixels; previously resized tiles keep their saved size.
