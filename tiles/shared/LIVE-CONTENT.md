# Reviewed classroom content

The unrestricted Wiktionary and Wikiquote feeds are removed. Long words and keyword filters did not provide a reliable classroom review.

The source of displayed definitions is now the TeacherTiles classroom catalog: 365 individually authored, plain-language definitions. Cambridge Dictionary links are lookup references, not a claim that Cambridge supplied or licensed these definitions. Vocabulary covers useful academic language and science. The default All collection gives 365 distinct daily selections; the optional collections contain 183 and 182 words and repeat sooner. Another previews the next entry and does not create an infinite stream.

The quote catalog contains 20 approved public-domain excerpts about learning, effort, curiosity and friendship. Novel dialogue is credited to the author and work. The collection is deliberately finite; it repeats after 20 days. No raw upstream quote is eligible for automatic display. Expansion requires reviewing the exact quotation, author, source and classroom context.

Each catalog.json is fetched from this site's own origin daily. The bundled words.js and quotes.js contain the matching offline entries and form an exact-content allowlist; altered or unknown entries cannot appear through a catalog or cached response. Update both JSON and its bundled counterpart together after reviewing additions. Content refresh retrieves published catalog revisions, not automatically generated or externally sourced unreviewed entries. The reviewed-v1 cache replaces and ignores the previous open-feed caches.

Google opens normal results in a separate tab with SafeSearch active. New tiles are 560 by 190 pixels; previously resized tiles keep their saved size.
