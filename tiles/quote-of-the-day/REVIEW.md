# Quote collection review

The collection contains 365 distinct excerpts. The first 20 retain the previously reviewed entries. Additions were selected individually from the sources below; downloading these books is not part of the app's runtime. The app displays only exact approved catalog entries.

## Sources and attribution

- [Many Thoughts of Many Minds, compiled by Louis Klopsch (1896)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17112): selected quotations are linked to their page in the public-domain anthology. Their displayed source says “Collected in Many Thoughts of Many Minds (1896).” Attribution follows the printed collection, with familiar abbreviated author names expanded where identifiable. This verifies what the anthology prints, not that every attribution has been independently traced to the author's manuscript or original publication. Historical anthologies can contain attribution errors. Do not describe these entries as authenticated transcripts.
- [It Can Be Done: Poems of Inspiration](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10763): selected complete stanzas are credited to their individual poets, not the anthology's compilers. Links point to the stanza. “Etta Wheeler Wilcox,” a transcription error in the Life section, is corrected to Ella Wheeler Wilcox. Line breaks and repeated whitespace are collapsed for the tile; verse wording is retained.
- [Emerson's Essays](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16643): selected complete sentences from the essays, excluding the editor's introduction and notes, with links to the essay. Page numbers and footnote markers are removed.
- The retained entries link to Little Women, Anne of Green Gables, Walden, The Prophet, Emerson's Essays and Franklin's Autobiography.

## Student-facing selection

The review favors learning, independent thought, generosity, friendship, creativity, patience and constructive effort. It excludes the source collections' explicit religious instruction, threats of punishment, sexual content, slurs, violent imagery, assertions that illness or poverty is a personal failure, and passages that tell a struggling student not to seek help. Historical wording and ordinary literary metaphors remain; these are quotations, not factual lessons or health advice. Source books contain material outside the selected excerpts and are not themselves curated children's editions.

Quotes were checked as standalone entries, rather than approved by positive keywords alone. The 365 entries have unique normalized text; excerpts are not generated slogans or padded variants of a small set of quotes. Tests verify catalog/bundle agreement, reject altered entries, and walk full daily cycles. These tests protect approved content and rotation; they do not independently prove historical attribution or replace editorial review.
