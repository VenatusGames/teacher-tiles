# TeacherTiles v1.5.0 - The PBIS Update

### The PBIS Update
**MAJOR CONTENT UPDATE**
This update focuses on different PBIS and student incentive tools! Reward students with in-app tools or print our real-world incentives with in-app tools. This is the first-wave of PBIS related tools. There are more coming in future updates. Pick and choose what PBIS tools you like to use. Future updates will allow coordination across large organizations and groups.
