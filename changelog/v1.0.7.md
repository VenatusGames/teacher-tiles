# TeacherTiles v1.1

Our biggest update yet!

## New Features
- Overhauled visual timers
- Setup for Themes, Stickers, and Shop
- Expanded canvas work area. Place modules off screen, snap to sides of screen, zoom in and out with scroll wheel. Explore the entire new board! Have different areas for different things and easily use scroll wheel to find your place. Middle click and drag to navigate across the board too!
- Hid UI buttons until mouse is in the area. They fade in and out as needed.
- Added Tile Search Function
- Added three new Tile Categories: Math, Literacy & SEL

## New Tiles
- Tons of New Tiles! 
* Visual Schedule Tile - Create a visual schedule for your day! 
* Progress Bar Tile - Track progress toward a specific time of your day or completion of an activity!
* Date Tile - Shows current date with lots of options.
* Calendar Tile - Fully functional calendar
* Group Maker Tile - Create groups or partners and shuffle them with ease!
* Lunch Count Tile - Take tallys of what people want for lunch with various food options or input & drag actual student names into the categories
* Voting Tile - Take polls and votes in realtime of student input!
* Ambience Player Tile - Various ambient videos for classroom zen or rest time
* Wordy Puzzle Tile - a game tile that allows the teacher to create Wordy Puzzles
* Number Line Tile - A number line tool
* Hundreds Chart Tile - a Hundreds chart tile
* Ten Frames Tile - a ten frames tile with manipulatives
* Money Tile - Money counting manipulatives for learning and practicing money
* Calculator Tile - Self Explanatory! A calculator! 
* Periodic Table Tile - an interactive periodic table of elements
* Graphing Tool Tile - A basic implementation of an early graphing tool. More improvements to follow.
* Ruler Tile - a functional ruler
* ABC Tile - Practice uppercase and lowercase letter fluency.
* CVC Tile - A CVC wordbank of CVC flashcards for quick & easy early level CVC word practice.
* High Frequency Word Tile - K-5 HFW Fluency Practice. Turn off certain words if you haven't taught them yet in the settings.
