# TeacherTiles v2.0.0 - Public Beta Launch Version!

![TeacherTiles Screenshot](https://i.imgur.com/lDLaBKJ.jpeg)
## TeacherTiles Public Beta Launch Version
TeacherTiles has officially launched in Beta to the public! Share us with your friends! 
This is our largest update to date. It includes over 40 new tiles, our brand new Lesson Planning app, **tons of new content & tools**, and Paid Features are now live! Board Templates are also live! This update also adds basic support for future features like Organizations and many bugs were fixed & outdated features were improved. The entire app has recieved a visual and clarity overhaul. Check it out!

## New Features

- **Subscriptions are live!** Support TeacherTiles by subscribing and unlock lots of perks, with more on the way!
- Purchase themes, stickers, skins, cursors, and other cosmetics to help keep TeacherTiles free and online!
- **Tile Skinning System:** Click the skins button in a tile to change its skin. Plenty of skins are already available, with more to come!
- Added more stickers.
- Improved the shop experience.
- **Board Frames:** Save up to five specific zoomed-in spots or viewing angles on your board and quickly return to them. Perfect for using one board for multiple activities and moving between them with ease. Hold **F** to open the frame menu!
- **In-App User Support:** Report bugs and get help with purchases or other issues directly in the app.
- **Board Templates:** Explore community-made boards, download them for your own use, and upload your own creations!
- Set a nickname for your board template profile so other users know you by it.
- **Tile Tabbing:** Create multiple tabs within a tile or combine tiles into one to quickly switch between tile presets.
- **Fullscreen Tiles:** Go fullscreen with any tile!
- **Tile Pinning:** Pin a tile to your camera so it follows you anywhere on your board.
- **Tile Locking:** Lock a tile to prevent it from being moved or snapped to.
- **Lesson Planning Tool:** The new lesson planner is now available!
- Create planners and plan lessons directly in the app. View your plans at a glance with a board tile, then open the planner for more in-depth editing.
- Added planner skins.
- Added profile frames.
- Press **Spacebar** to reset to the default zoom.
- Use the mini-map to help find your way while navigating the board.
- Improved the Draw Tile.
- The notification interface now stores organization invites and other notifications.
- **Organizations:** Added the basic foundation for organizations.
- Invite people to join your organization, manage members, and remove members when needed.
- In a future update, organizations will support sharing classes, PBIS, live collaboration boards, and more!
- Letter Flashcards now highlight vowels.
- Made lots of improvements across all flashcard-style tiles.
- Adjust segment sizing in the Visual Schedule.
- Attendance Tiles now use magnet-style student names, with support for other student-name tiles coming soon!
- **New Interactive Timers:** Try the Rocket and Sunflower timers!
- Added an animation when purchasing a coin pack.
- Added many new tile categories!
- Added the Class Connect category, featuring Class Connect Tiles that are coming soon!
- Add borders to your tiles to customize their look and feel.
- **Timer Sync:** Sync all timers on your board.
- Added groundwork for upcoming holiday features.
- Added merge-conflict save functionality.
- Disable tile headings in the tile settings.
- **New Patch Achievements:** Most patches are not unlockable yet, but will be soon. All users at the time of this update's release receive the **Alpha Tester patch** in celebration of moving on to Beta! Thanks for helping us grow!
- Search settings and help to find what you need.
- Toggle on the timer finish-time display.
