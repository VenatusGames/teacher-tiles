PRODUCTIVE SCREEN TIME

Screen time,
with a reason.

Give screen time an explicit purpose. Our focus is on avoiding screen fatigue and passive viewing: use the tools that support a clear learning or classroom goal, then make room for learning beyond the screen.

01

A clear job for every tool.

Teach a skill. Explain a task. Guide a routine. Choose tools for what they help your class accomplish.

02

Simple from the start.

Begin with just what you need. Add more when it helps. Your board grows when you choose.

03

You set the visual pace.

Themes, animations, and busier graphics are optional. You decide what belongs in your classroom.
