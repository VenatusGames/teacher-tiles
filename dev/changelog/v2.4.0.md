## TeacherTiles 2.4 Update - ClassConnect & More! 
It's here! Thanks for being so patient while we worked on this massive systems update. With this release comes lots of new content and features including the long awaited ClassConnect. Teachers can now create boards for their students to use via the Class View Tool or directly on the board with tiles. Establish a link for students via the ClassConnect button inside the class page and students can join a session whenever it is active or started. We will have more tutorials and clearer direction for ClassConnect in coming updates. This update introduces the core functionality! Please send us your feedback and ideas! We are excited to continue improving TeacherTiles for your use!
